## Users

**Primary visitors:** Owners and decision-makers at small and mid-sized service businesses—marketing agencies, creative/video studios, consultants, coaches, B2B sales teams, and professional-service firms.

**Job to be done:** Determine whether Symbiosis can replace their fragmented CRM, spreadsheets, proposal tools, project management, calendars, finance trackers, and AI subscriptions stack. They need to know if it simplifies operations, speeds up lead response and proposals, clarifies profitability, integrates AI without tool sprawl, and provides immediate ROI.

**Secondary visitors:** Performance-oriented agencies, high-ticket coaches, consultants, and B2B growth teams who use video, webinars, VSLs, or paid social in their sales process.

**Job to be done:** Evaluate Symbiosis Cinema specifically—the content-to-revenue layer. They want to create on-brand AI video, understand engagement, connect content activity to leads and deals, and receive actionable next-step recommendations.

## Product Purpose

Symbiosis OS is an AI-powered business operating system for service businesses. It unifies CRM and pipeline management, proposals and pricing, prospecting, client briefs, project management, finance visibility, calendar/booking, automations, analytics, and AI assistance in one connected workspace.

Symbiosis Cinema is the platform's content-to-revenue studio. It enables businesses to build a brand profile, create reusable AI characters and assets, generate campaign content, publish it, track engagement, connect content behavior to CRM leads, and receive AI-powered recommendations for follow-up timing, sales messaging, and offers.

**Success:** Service businesses move from operating across ten disconnected tools to running sales, content, client delivery, and revenue from one connected system. This results in faster client acquisition, clearer profitability, less manual work, and measurable ROI.

## Operating Context

**The fragmentation problem:** Service businesses currently use a CRM, spreadsheets, proposal tools, project management, calendars, finance trackers, and multiple AI subscriptions. Data does not flow between systems. Lead status, proposal outcomes, project delivery, and content engagement remain disconnected. Follow-ups are missed. Profitability is unclear. Team coordination is manual and error-prone.

**The connected flow Symbiosis enables:**  
Content → engagement → lead → proposal → project → revenue

Every interaction becomes useful context for the next action. AI recommendations become smarter as operational data accumulates.

**Use cases:**
- A marketing agency creates AI video content, sees engagement metrics, connects it to qualified leads in the CRM, and receives a recommendation to call a prospect who watched a VSL three times.
- A consultant receives an inbound inquiry, generates a proposal with AI-assisted pricing tiers, tracks client response, and measures project profitability back to the original marketing source.
- A creative studio manages client briefs, project timelines, team workload, and finances in one view—and sees which content campaigns led to the most profitable client relationships.

## Product Principles

1. **Connected, not fragmented.** Every piece of the business—content, leads, proposals, projects, revenue—shares the same context. Information flows; data does not hide in silos.

2. **Designed for service businesses, not generic teams.** The language, workflows, and outcomes center on clients, proposals, delivery, team workload, and revenue—not abstract tasks or enterprise software complexity.

3. **AI is a workflow partner, not a separate subscription.** AI assistance should feel like one operating system, not another tool to configure, connect, and maintain.

4. **Guidance before features.** Non-technical business owners should feel guided through implementation, setup, and first wins—not overwhelmed by options or API keys.

5. **Proof comes from connected data.** The long-term competitive advantage is not merely a list of features; it is the cumulative operational and attribution data that makes each recommendation smarter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Landing page: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance minimum (headings, alt text, keyboard navigation, color contrast, screen reader support).
- Content language: plain English and Bulgarian. Avoid jargon; define technical terms.
- Product CRM and proposal sections: Ensure team members with diverse abilities can access lead records, send messages, and manage workflows.
- Video and audio content: Provide captions, transcripts, and audio descriptions.
- Forms and input: Clear labels, error messages, and validation feedback.
